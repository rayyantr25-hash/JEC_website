Jasky Engineering Company focuses on practical engineering, steel fabrication and metalwork for demanding real-world applications.

01 / WHO WE ARE

Serious about steel.

JEC is an industrial engineering and fabrication company based in Shershah, Karachi. Our work spans precision cutting, metal shaping, steel fabrication, parts assembly, industrial machinery work and custom steel solutions.

We approach every requirement from the perspective of how the finished work will actually be used — balancing strength, accuracy, practicality and finish.

02 / WHAT WE DO

From raw material to finished work.

Our capabilities cover the full fabrication journey: understanding requirements, preparing materials, cutting and shaping, joining and assembly, finishing and preparing the final piece for its environment.

We also develop steel furniture and industrial storage products including workbenches, cabinets, trolleys, lockers, racks, stainless steel tables, office furniture and industrial seating.

03 / OUR APPROACH

Precision is a habit.

Good fabrication is more than joining metal together. It is about understanding dimensions, tolerances, load, access, finish and the environment where the finished work will live.

That mindset shapes how JEC approaches custom work: clear requirements, practical decisions, disciplined fabrication and attention to the final result.
